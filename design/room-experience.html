The room experience to build

  • Portrait: video above the people row, conversation and message box. The keyboard must leave the message box usable.
  • Full screen is a personal choice: one viewer can hide chat while the other keeps chatting. Returning to portrait restores that viewer’s conversation and unsent draft.
  • Readiness and reconnection: show who is loading. Seek to the host’s current timeline after rejoining, rather than replaying old commands.
  • Distance: each phone receives its own provider stream. Orbit exchanges timing, readiness and room events; it does not relay the movie.

This design does not choose a player integration. A native in-app player gives Orbit control of the layout. A floating companion depends on Android and the streaming app’s window behavior; it may cover video rather than reserve space below it.
